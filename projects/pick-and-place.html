Autonomous Systems

Autonomous Pick-and-Place Robot

Developed an Arduino-based pick-and-place mechanism with ultrasonic sensing, a DC motor and PID control for a smooth approach to the object.

DeveloperJanuary – June 2025
PID motor controlAutomatic gripper

Development process

01

Control development

Worked in a six-person team on an aluminium-frame mechanism with one degree of freedom, an Arduino, a DC motor and an ultrasonic sensor.

02

Data & tuning

Configured the ultrasonic sensor and debugged the mathematical model used to approach an object. PID control smoothed the approach; the build did not use encoders or autonomous task sequencing.

03

Mechanical gripper

Designed a gripper that automatically closed around objects once the robot reached the target distance.

04

Build documentation

The CAD model and assembled robot photographs below document the mechanical system. A detailed test log and demonstration video are still to be added.
