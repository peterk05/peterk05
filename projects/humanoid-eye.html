Autonomous Humanoid Eye

Camera-driven animatronic eye system that detects and tracks a person's position in real time, physically moving the eye mechanism to follow them around the room. Computer vision pipeline on Raspberry Pi drives servo-actuated eye movement with smooth, continuous tracking.

Development process

01

Objective

Build an animatronic eye that detects a person and physically follows their position around a room.

02

Perception

A camera feeds a computer vision pipeline running on Raspberry Pi. OpenCV and Python provide the processing used to detect and track the person’s position.

03

Actuation

The tracking output drives a servo-actuated eye mechanism, translating visual position into physical movement.

04

System behaviour

The homepage describes smooth, continuous tracking in real time. Quantitative test results and build photographs have not yet been published.
