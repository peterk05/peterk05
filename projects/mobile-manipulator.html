Development process

01

Project brief

Develop a low-cost mobile manipulator for an international robotics hackathon.

02

Arm fabrication

Built four functional SO-101 arms with carbon-fibre printed parts: two leader arms and two follower arms for simultaneous operation. Each arm uses six ST3215 servos.

03

Teleoperation

Implemented Python-based teleoperation for the leader/follower arms. An Intel RealSense camera is part of the hardware setup.

04

Autonomous sorting

Autonomous sorting is planned. The vision model has not yet been integrated, and no autonomous sorting results are claimed.
