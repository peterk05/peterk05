Combat Robotics

RoboRiotz Combat Robot

Led full system architecture of a featherweight combat robot. Secured €2,000 in independent sponsorship. Applied MATLAB modelling to optimise rotational dynamics, achieving a 35% increase in rotational force and 95% competition reliability.

Development process

01

Concept & research

The team investigated torque-reaction weapon systems and reviewed existing combat robot designs. Rapid motor reversal was selected to rotate the robot around fixed wheels and deliver a strike through angular momentum.

02

Modelling & component selection

MATLAB models explored angular momentum transfer, rotational dynamics and achievable force. The modelling informed motor selection and provided a way to evaluate the weapon concept before fabrication.

03

Power & mechanical integration

The existing project documentation describes a 6S LiPo power system, electronic speed controllers and a steel frame within the 12 kg featherweight limit. Integration balanced impact resistance, weight distribution and drivetrain responsiveness.

04

Build, test & iterate

The five-person team divided fabrication and integration work across the four-month build. Iterative testing identified mechanical stress points and motor heating, leading to changes in thermal management and operating duty cycles.

05

Outcome & lessons

The homepage reports €2,000 in sponsorship, a 35% increase in rotational force and 95% competition reliability. The project brought together mathematical modelling, system integration and team coordination.
